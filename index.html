Welcome to the Club

More Than
Just Rugby

Hollybush RFC is a proud community rugby club competing in the WRU Admiral National League Division 5 East. We're a tight-knit club with a big heart, built on passion, grit, and a love of the beautiful game.

Whether you're a seasoned player looking for a new home, a youngster picking up a ball for the first time, or a supporter who just wants to get behind a real local club — you're welcome at Hollybush.

We play our home games with pride, train hard all week, and take care of each other on and off the pitch. Come join the family.
